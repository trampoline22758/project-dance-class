Summer at a Glance

We blend a playful half-day camp with weekly evening classes so dancers can stay inspired all summer long.

Half-Day Dance Camp

  • One week in late June—final dates shared each spring
  • Morning session for ages 3–5 · Afternoon session for ages 6–8
  • Creative movement, ballet & tap, tumbling, crafts, and friends

Evening Classes

  • Ballet, jazz, hip-hop, pom-poms, tap, musical theater & more
  • Flexible drop-in options for vacation schedules
  • Small groups with lots of individual attention
